UMEP Lab is pleased to announce an online training session presented by **Dr. Qiumeng Li**, Assistant Professor in the Thrust of Urban Governance and Design and the Thrust of Intelligent Transportation at HKUST(GZ).

## Session details

- **Date:** Tuesday, September 29, 2026
- **China:** 18:00 (UTC+8)
- **India:** 15:30 (UTC+5:30)
- **United Kingdom:** 11:00 (BST, UTC+1)
- **Format:** Online via Zoom
- **Campus venue:** Live from Room 103, E1, HKUST(GZ) Campus

The session explores how AI agents can help researchers understand human mobility and support healthier, more sustainable cities. It is presented with the Urban Studies Foundation as part of the *Mapping Opportunities in Post-Industrial Cities: Healthy and Sustainable Urban Transitions in Post-Industrial Cities* initiative.
